## Source caveats worth keeping in the knowledge base

- His [protocol document](https://github.com/Bukczyk/CP2077-Coop/blob/20eb125796a4a5e55394cc2c3ed344f9b8456b4a/docs/PROTOCOL.md) still lists two members and 1024 entities per session, but the [current header](https://github.com/Bukczyk/CP2077-Coop/blob/20eb125796a4a5e55394cc2c3ed344f9b8456b4a/shared/include/coop/session.hpp) defaults to **16 members and 4096 entities**, with member/entity maps. This is a documentation/code mismatch; neither limit proves a live 16-player session.
- His [network header](https://github.com/Bukczyk/CP2077-Coop/blob/20eb125796a4a5e55394cc2c3ed344f9b8456b4a/shared/include/coop/net.hpp) includes **length-prefixed TCP channels plus UDP helpers**. The update's UDP description should not be treated as a verified description of the complete new transport. Compare reliability, reconnect and head-of-line behavior before adopting any transport design.
- His [interpolation source](https://github.com/Bukczyk/CP2077-Coop/blob/20eb125796a4a5e55394cc2c3ed344f9b8456b4a/shared/src/interpolation.cpp) has delayed sampling, bounded extrapolation, a capped sample history and shortest-angle Euler blending. Useful comparison cases, but not a validated cure for our puppet steering or a replacement for the full-quaternion vehicle requirement.
- His architecture audit includes historical statements such as absent root build/runtime import that later commits resolved. Prefer pinned current source and dated migration evidence over treating every paragraph as current status.
